A method, not a verdict

The line matters.
So does its label.

A board position, investment, invitation and criminal conviction establish different things. The atlas keeps those distinctions visible, links claims to their sources, and identifies incomplete coverage.

Shared attendance does not establish an alliance. A name in an Epstein document does not establish wrongdoing. A historical event does not establish an unbroken chain to the present.
